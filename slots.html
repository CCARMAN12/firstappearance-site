Why six and not sixty

Because what is being sold is the absence of competitors. A seventh DUI subscriber would be buying letters that do not exist — the roster produces what it produces, and splitting it is the one thing that would make it worthless.

One arrest produces one letter across every firm we work with. Which firm gets it is decided by the lead charge on the booking, before anything is printed, so no arrested person ever receives two letters from us.

This board is the proof. Scarcity nobody can check is just a sentence in a sales letter. This is the same page everyone sees, it changes when a slot goes, and a slot sitting open here for months is evidence we are not inventing demand.

How the figures were measured

Arrest volumes are from the Florida Department of Law Enforcement's Uniform Crime Report for 2024. The mailable share is the proportion of Pinellas booking records carrying a usable mailing address, counted directly off the roster. The price is the same for every slot and is not derived from these volumes — a DUI slot produces more than a traffic slot, and costs the same. That is deliberate: one price is a straight answer, and a tier list is a negotiation.

No arrested person is named here or on any page we publish. An arrest is a public record, which is not the same as something that should be republished with a name on it.
